WDD 131 Project Site Plan

Lift to Grow

This website will explain the basics of muscle growth in a simple way for beginners who want to understand how to build muscle better.

Site Name

Lift to Grow is the name I chose because it is short, easy to remember, and clearly connects to the idea of building muscle through lifting weights.

Purpose

The purpose of this site is to teach beginners how hypertrophy works and show the main things that help with muscle growth such as training, nutrition, and rest.

Audience

The target audience is beginners in the gym, students, and people who want simple information about muscle growth without too much technical language.

Dynamic Features

I plan to include JavaScript features like an interactive section for common myths, a simple form for beginner recommendations, and some basic user interaction to make the site more useful.

Scenarios

  • How do muscles grow after resistance training?
  • What should a beginner focus on first to build muscle?
  • What mistakes can slow down muscle growth?

Planned Pages

Home

This page will introduce the topic and give a general overview of hypertrophy and why it matters.

How Growth Works

This page will explain how muscles grow, including the role of resistance training, recovery, and progressive overload.

Mistakes & Recovery

This page will talk about common mistakes beginners make, like overtraining or not eating enough, and explain why recovery is important.

Tips for Beginners

This page will give practical and simple tips that beginners can follow to improve their results in the gym.

Typography

I will use Bebas Neue for headings because it has a strong gym style, and Manrope for the body text because it is easy to read.

Wireframe Notes

  • Mobile view will have the content stacked in one column with simple navigation.
  • Desktop view will have more space, so sections can be wider and some content can be shown in columns.
